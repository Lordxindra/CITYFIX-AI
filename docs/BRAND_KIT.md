# CityFix AI brand kit

- **Name:** CityFix AI
- **Descriptor:** The intelligence layer for better cities.
- **Tagline:** Report Less. Resolve More.
- **Personality:** Trustworthy, civic-minded, clear, calm and action-oriented.
- **Primary background:** Midnight navy `#0B1020`
- **Surface:** `#111A2E`
- **Primary accent:** Violet `#A78BFA`
- **Secondary accent:** Emerald `#34D399`
- **Attention:** Amber `#FBBF24`
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk for headings; DM Sans for interface text.
- **Logo direction:** A simple C-shaped civic pin with a small plus sign; do not use government seals or official municipal marks.
- **Voice:** Plain language, transparent about what is a prototype, never promise official resolution.
