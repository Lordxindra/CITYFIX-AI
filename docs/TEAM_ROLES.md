# Suggested team roles

The problem statement asks for a mix of developers, designers, video editors and a wildcard role (writer, marketer, researcher or extra builder), with one team lead as the organiser contact.

| Role | Responsibilities | Evidence to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Team lead / product owner | Scope, schedule, final submission, organiser communication | Submission checklist and final link verification |
| Developer 1 | Frontend, accessibility, responsive UI, map and report flow | Live product walkthrough |
| Developer 2 | Supabase schema, auth, storage, Edge Functions and security | Architecture explanation and backend demo |
| Designer | Brand kit, logo, landing page, UX and post templates | Brand kit PDF and design rationale |
| Video/content + research wildcard | Launch film, explainer, interviews, captions, social posting | Videos, anonymised interview notes and post links |

Adapt these roles to the actual registered team. Do not list people who have not agreed to participate or are not eligible.
