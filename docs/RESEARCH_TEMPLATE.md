# CityFix AI — resident problem validation

Do not fill this with invented results. Use this template during real interviews or observations.

## Research question
How do residents in selected Jaipur neighbourhoods notice, report and follow up on public-space issues such as road damage, waste overflow, waterlogging and broken streetlights?

## Suggested method
- Conduct 8–12 short interviews across more than one neighbourhood.
- Ask permission before taking notes or recording.
- Do not collect names, phone numbers, exact home addresses or sensitive personal information unless genuinely required and consented to.
- Record only broad area and anonymised participant code.
- Separate participant quotes, observations and the team's assumptions.

## Interview questions
1. What public-space issue have you noticed most often recently?
2. What do you currently do when you notice it?
3. What makes reporting or following up difficult?
4. How do you know whether a report has been acknowledged or resolved?
5. Would a map and visible status history help? Why or why not?
6. What information would you be comfortable sharing in a public report?
7. What would make you trust or distrust a civic reporting tool?
