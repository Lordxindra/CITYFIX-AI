# Problem validation plan

Do not invent survey results. Complete this short validation before claiming local impact.

## Interview prompts
1. What public-space issue do you notice most often in your neighbourhood?
2. How do you currently report it, if at all?
3. What makes reporting difficult?
4. How do you know whether anything happened after reporting?
5. Would a map and transparent status history help? Why or why not?
6. What information would you be comfortable sharing? What should remain private?

## Suggested sample
Speak with 8–12 residents across at least two neighbourhoods. Record date, broad area, role (resident/shopkeeper/commuter), and anonymised notes. Ask permission before recording names or audio.
