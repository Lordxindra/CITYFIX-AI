# Product overview

## Problem
Residents often notice potholes, overflowing waste, broken streetlights and waterlogging, but the information can be fragmented, difficult to track and hard for communities to prioritise.

## Proposed solution
CityFix AI is a citizen-first issue intelligence prototype. It structures reports, displays them geographically, offers transparent triage suggestions and makes status changes visible.

## Primary users
- Residents reporting public-space issues
- Community volunteers reviewing local needs
- A future authorised operations team managing reports

## Core user journey
1. Resident describes an issue and location.
2. Resident optionally selects a photo for local preview.
3. A transparent rule-based triage suggests a priority.
4. The report appears in the issue list and map.
5. A demo admin changes its status.
6. Community points recognise participation.

## Differentiation
The product goes beyond a complaint form by combining a map, explainable prioritisation, status visibility and community participation in one experience.

## Non-goals for this prototype
- Claiming government affiliation
- Dispatching municipal workers
- Guaranteeing resolution timelines
- Collecting precise user location automatically
- Claiming a trained computer-vision model where none is connected
