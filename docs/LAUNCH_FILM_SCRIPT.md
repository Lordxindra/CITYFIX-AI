# 60–90 second launch film

**0–10s:** Quick cuts: road damage, overflowing bin, broken streetlight. On-screen: “A city is made of a million everyday moments.”

**10–22s:** A resident photographs a public-space issue. On-screen: “But when something goes wrong, where does the information go?”

**22–40s:** Product reveal: CityFix AI dashboard, report form, map markers. Voiceover: “CityFix AI turns local observations into structured, visible issue reports.”

**40–58s:** Show priority suggestion, issue table and status change. Voiceover: “Understand what needs attention, see progress and make community participation count.”

**58–75s:** Show community impact screen and product logo. Voiceover: “Not another complaint box. A clearer picture of what our neighbourhoods need.”

**75–85s:** CTA: “Explore the prototype. Share feedback. Build better cities together.”

Add event hashtags and organiser tag in the social caption. Use only original footage/assets or properly licensed material. Obtain permission before filming private spaces; blur faces and number plates unless consent is obtained.
